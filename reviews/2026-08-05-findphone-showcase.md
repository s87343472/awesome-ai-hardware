## English

| Candidate | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [findphone](https://github.com/ben-z/findphone) | Showcase only | The author demonstrates a Claude-assisted build process and a real Bluetooth-RSSI phone-finding result. The released Swift/macOS utility scans Bluetooth signal strength at runtime; it does not use AI, LLMs, or agents at runtime, and the repository has no declared license. |

The X post and its video are retained because they are a strong example of using an AI coding assistant to create a one-off tool that interacts with a physical device. It is not a formal catalog entry under the repository's AI-core and explicit-license requirements.
