## English

This section preserves the thinking, build process, trade-offs, and real-world demonstrations around AI hardware projects. Entries are organized by the X post's publication month, newest first.

A showcase is not automatically a catalog endorsement. Every entry states its relationship to the project and catalog status:

- **Author:** posted by the creator of the linked implementation.
- **Project team:** posted by a person presenting the team's own work.
- **Community:** third-party explanation or recommendation; claims are checked against the repository before entering the catalog.
- **Listed:** passes the formal open-source catalog rules.
- **Deferred:** relevant, but currently misses a mandatory requirement such as a license.
- **Showcase only:** useful as an idea or demonstration but not a formal open-source catalog entry.

Media remains on its original X, YouTube, or project-hosted page. This repository links to it and does not rehost unlicensed material.

## 简体中文

本栏目保存 AI 硬件项目背后的思路、制作过程、取舍和真实演示，并按照 X Post 的发布日期倒序整理。

进入 Showcase 不等于通过正式项目收录。每个条目都会标明与项目的关系和目录状态：

- **作者：** 帖子由关联实现的创作者发布。
- **项目团队：** 帖子由介绍团队自身工作的成员发布。
- **社区分享：** 第三方解读或推荐；相关声明在正式收录前会与仓库交叉核验。
- **已收录：** 通过正式开源项目规则。
- **暂缓：** 方向相关，但缺少许可证等强制条件。
- **仅 Showcase：** 思路或演示有价值，但不是正式开源目录条目。

媒体保留在原始 X、YouTube 或项目页面，本仓库只提供链接，不重新托管未经授权的素材。
